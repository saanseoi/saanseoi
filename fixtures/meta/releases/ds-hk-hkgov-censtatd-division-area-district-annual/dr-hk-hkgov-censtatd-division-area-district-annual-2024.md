# EN

## Changelog

- <orange>Upstream</orange> Add the 2024 District Council statistical geography
  published with C&SD's annual population and household statistics.

## Geometry

The source release selects the 18 `DC_GHS` features whose `year` is <black>2024</black>.
Their native <black>EPSG:2326</black> geometry is retained and projected to canonical
<black>EPSG:4326</black>. The exact companion is <black>areas:hkgov-censtatd</black> at
cohort <black>2024</black>; its topology-preserving 10-metre display transform is
<black>areas:hkgov-censtatd:simplified</black> at the same cohort.

## Compatibility

This source release belongs to C&SD's annual Population and Household Statistics by
District Council District publication. Statistics whose source features belong to this
2024 cohort select this companion for unqualified <black>include=areas</black>.
