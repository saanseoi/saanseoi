# EN

## Changelog

- Initial 山水 | SaanSeoi source release of the **Areas and Districts** appendix from
  the **Hong Kong Property Review 2015**, published by the Rating and Valuation
  Department on 30 April 2015.
- Retains 148 membership assertions: 3 areas, 18 districts, 126 subdistricts and the
  explicitly included locality of Tung Chung under Lantau Island.
- Supplies membership evidence to
  [Divisions, Localities, Neighbourhoods & Islands](/sources/ds-hk-saanseoi-division):
  147 assertions match curated divisions. Lei Muk Shue is excluded because its an
  historic vilalge which merely lends its name to an estate.

## Source evidence

The retained archive includes the bilingual publisher PDF, parsed records, checksum,
publication date and source URL. Records retain PDF page evidence, bilingual names,
listed tertiary planning units and partial-unit `(p)` markers. The separate Office
Sub-districts section is excluded from this collection.

## Interpretation

Membership describes inclusion in this historical publication. It establishes neither
statutory status nor current boundaries and does not supply geometry.
